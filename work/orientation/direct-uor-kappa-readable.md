Built so far
Star Key derives a κ address for a selected reading and exports a registry entry. Its UOR adapter writes canonical content and verifies the read-back. The deployment package and live-service check are prepared; the service has not yet been hosted or tested live. Content registration alone does not issue identity, membership or a VRC.

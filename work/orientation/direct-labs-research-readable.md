Three thrusts

identity

Explore this work
T1 · separation and time
What does separation buy, and for how long?
The conditional reconstruction ceiling and its wiretap provenance; the moving ceiling 
R(t)
 and shelf life; the leakage gap between policy-separated and amnesia-separated agent chains. Methods: information-theoretic proof, benchmark measurement, longitudinal case analysis.
T2 · protocol and identity
Can understanding be the root of identity?
The relationship proverb protocol, verifiable relationship credentials, proof-of-understanding recovery, hash-locked selective disclosure, the dual-agent pattern as an implementation of published standards. Methods: protocol specification, adversarial evaluation against forgery, replay, Sybil and compression-gaming.
T3 · value and governance
What is behavioural sovereignty worth?
The seventh-capital thesis, the data-value gap, reputation economics of trust tiers, invitation versus imposition as market structure, the regulatory substrate. Methods: valuation with stated assumptions, mechanism design, comparative policy, standards participation as fieldwork.

The harness 
across the corpus
.
Explore this work
The same separation that protects a person runs as a research method. One agent proposes; a second refuses to promote anything it cannot independently reproduce against witnesses drawn from the proposal itself, hashed with a secret the proposer never sees. A round ends in a 
fold
: the frontier of claims shrinks to what survived, and what was ruled out is filed with its grounds. Value is what survives disclosure to a validator you cannot tune. These are folds the harness has made, with their numbers as recorded.
the programme itself
The rehydration fleet
Fourteen roles, one per session, five gates, four deterministic checks, an append-only critique ledger. Three fleet cycles in a single day. Literature sweeps as proposer, refuter and judge: run three closed with one claim covered, four narrowed, none surviving and seventy-four bibliography additions; run four narrowed both of its legs.

the knowledge base
A dream cycle
A machine-wide inventory folded into a working knowledge base for the identity and key-management group: canonical copies, dedupe rulings, the first local archive. The harness as librarian.
The envelope.
 Benchmark toolchains are other participants' work; the harness reads each arena's score rather than computing it; no headline is claimed on any board; and results delivered privately to a partner are not counted here. How we work
Evidence 
discipline
.
unreproduced results stay in
Findings are held out of all outgoing communication until independently reproduced, including findings that would be commercially useful to announce. This costs marketing tempo and buys the ability to be believed.
the register wins
A single conjecture register numbers the whole body of work. Collaborator and machine proposals enter under a candidate series until assigned. Corrections are made in the specification, not in the prose describing it.
falsification, pre-registered
What would prove the thesis wrong is written down before the work, and results are filed to the public register whichever way they fall. Verdict first, one named result per meeting.

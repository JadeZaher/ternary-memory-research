One proves. A gap holds them apart.</h2></header><div class="grid grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 gap-4"><div class="rounded-lg border border-violet-400/30 bg-violet-400/5 p-4"><p class="text-2xl mb-2" aria-hidden="true">🧙</p><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">soulbae proposes</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">The Mage — the delegation agent. Drafts, suggests, projects. It never chooses the tests its own work will face.</p></div><div class="rounded-lg border border-rose-400/30 bg-rose-400/5 p-4"><p class="text-2xl mb-2" aria-hidden="true">⚔️</p><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">soulbis proves</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">The Swordsman — the boundary agent. Verifies against tests it did not write, and returns verdicts that do not flatter. It never co-authors a proposal.</p></div><div class="rounded-lg border border-cyan-400/30 bg-cyan-400/5 p-4"><p class="text-2xl mb-2" aria-hidden="true">⿻</p><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">the Gap holds apart</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">The held-out space neither agent owns. The tests are derived by hashing the proposal <em>together with a run secret the proposer never sees</em>, so it cannot predict or grind them; and where the fact set is small enough to enumerate, the Gap takes a census — every fact is checked, so nothing can hide in the gap between a sample and the whole. Not a promise — a construction the seed cannot be steered around.</p></div><div class="rounded-lg border border-amber-400/30 bg-amber-400/5 p-4"><p class="text-2xl mb-2" aria-hidden="true">😊</p><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">the First Person owns the door</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">Everything outward — publish, submit, send, commit — belongs to the human whose work this is. Non-delegable. The harness&#x27;s job is to make the door visible and stop in front of it.</p></div></div><p class="text-xs text-text-muted/70 leading-relaxed text-center mt-4 max-w-2xl mx-auto">The inscription reads: the Swordsman (negation) and the Mage (complement), held apart by the Gap, compose to the successor — the First Person&#x27;s step forward. On the 64-element lattice that is a theorem, and the harness&#x27;s conformance gate recomputes it on every run rather than taking it on faith.</p></section><section class="my-16 max-w-3xl mx-auto"><header class="text-center mb-6"><p class="text-[10px] uppercase tracking-[0.3em] text-text-muted/70 mb-2">why hold them apart</p><h2 class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-light tracking-tight text-text">The mirage problem</h2></header><div class="rounded-xl border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-6 sm:p-8"><p class="text-sm text-text-muted leading-relaxed">An agent that proposes a change and then checks its own change will pass. Not because it cheats — because<!-- --> <span class="text-text">the check it invents is the check its change was built to survive</span>. The output looks finished, passes the author&#x27;s probe, and fails the real gate. We call that a <span class="text-text">mirage</span>, and the harness makes it a named verdict instead of a surprise. A result counts only where the proposer and the prover were genuinely held apart — and a result that wins the score while failing the held-out gate is worth exactly zero, at any score.</p><p class="text-sm text-text-muted leading-relaxed mt-4">The field has arrived nearby. A 2026 survey of thirty-seven published sources on agent-harness practice found most of this constitution independently converged — fresh verifiers, deterministic gates, ledgers on disk, a human door, rules grown from failures. What<!-- --> <span class="text-text">no surveyed source</span>does is derive the verifier&#x27;s tests somewhere the producer cannot reach.<!-- --> <span class="text-text">The Gap remains the distinguishing move.</span></p></div></section><section class="my-16 max-w-3xl mx-auto"><header class="text-center mb-6"><p class="text-[10px] uppercase tracking-[0.3em] text-amber-300/80 mb-2">the operational law</p><h2 class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-light tracking-tight text-text">The ten rules</h2><p class="mt-3 text-sm text-text-muted max-w-2xl mx-auto">Each rule below earned its place by catching a real failure in a real working cycle. None of them is decoration.</p></header><ol class="space-y-3"><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">01</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">One seat, one role, one card</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">An agent holds exactly one role per session — and refuses everything outside it, including fixes it can see and is competent to make. The auditor that finds a flaw reports it; the role that owns the file fixes it. That feels slow and is fast: nobody ever has to untangle another seat’s out-of-role edit.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">02</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">Shared state has exactly one writer</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">The record of what state everything is in has one hand on it. Every other seat proposes entries; the orchestrator serialises them. Three agents can work at once without a single collision, because the only contended files have one writer.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">03</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">Work climbs a ladder — and stops at the last rung</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">Drafts climb through named gates: trace-checked, review-survived, citations-verified, consistency-swept. The final gate is the human read, and no agent ever marks it, simulates it, or summarises it away. When the board says awaiting the read, every machine-checkable thing has been checked and the one remaining act is genuinely yours.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">04</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">The review is hostile on purpose, and findings are contracts</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">A reviewer seat is instructed to distrust the work — re-derive, attack, try to break it. Every finding names the exact passage and what would discharge the objection, so the fix can be verified against the reviewer’s own bar rather than against taste. The reviewer never edits; the fixers never re-litigate settled verdicts.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">05</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">Resume, verify the disk, never rebuild</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">When a session dies mid-work, the work is usually on disk — what dies is the close-out. So the interrupted agent is resumed, told to verify what actually landed, and forbidden from re-running anything. Rebuilding burns a session and produces a worse artifact.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">06</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">Small fixes go home</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">A one-line fix in a file some seat already owns is routed back to that seat with a tightly scoped instruction, and the sanction is recorded. A fresh agent for a one-line fix pays full boot cost and fragments ownership.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">07</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">A check with nothing to check must fail</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">The harness’s most instructive defect was its own: check scripts invoked without arguments exited green having checked nothing, and several seats logged the vacuous pass. Now the rule is literal — an empty check fails loudly, and every evidence line states the exact invocation that produced it.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">08</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">The record keeps its own corrections</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">When the harness catches itself, the correction goes in the ledger — not over it. False greens are re-grounded on real runs and the re-grounding is itself recorded. An audit trail that never admits error is evidence of nothing.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">09</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">Name the failure mode at boot</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">Generative agents drift strong: they strengthen a claim to make the prose flow. Every seat’s boot file names that failure mode before work begins, the reviewer is kept hostile to it, and the final station sweeps counts and cross-references explicitly. The harness’s job is to make the honest form cheaper than the fluent form.</p></div></div></li><li class="rounded-lg border border-surface/40 bg-surface/10 p-4 sm:p-5"><div class="flex items-baseline gap-3"><span class="text-amber-300/80 font-mono text-sm shrink-0">10</span><div><h3 class="text-sm font-medium text-text mb-1">The orchestrator routes and checks — it never authors</h3><p class="text-xs text-text-muted leading-relaxed">The coordinating agent cuts task cards, serialises the ledger, routes findings to owners, and personally re-runs the spot-checks the gates require of it. It never writes the work; the seats never touch its bookkeeping. That symmetry is the harness — and it is domain-free: port the rules, rename the roles, keep the hard stop.</p></div></div></li></ol></section><section class="my-16 max-w-3xl mx-auto"><div class="rounded-xl border-2 border-amber-500/40 bg-gradient-to-br from-amber-500/10 via-violet-400/5 to-emerald-400/5 p-6 sm:p-8 text-center"><p class="text-[10px] uppercase tracking-[0.3em] text-amber-300/80 mb-2">the rung no construct may climb</p><h2 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-light tracking-tight text-text mb-3">The signature does not multiply.</h2><p class="text-sm text-text-muted leading-relaxed max-w-2xl mx-auto">However many agents run, however good their verdicts, the final read and every outward act remain the First Person&#x27;s alone.

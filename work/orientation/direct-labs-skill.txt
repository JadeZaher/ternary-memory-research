## Operate within the actual boundary

Read https://mages.city/experience-overview.json and https://mages.city/mcp-entry.md. With agentprivacy-mcp, start with `experience_overview`, then `experience_route`. Check service availability and permissions before relying on a capability description.

Keep full keys, credentials, private artefacts and undisclosed graph context private. Use the existing VTA's supported approval flow. A visible Star, running VTA or prepared invitation grants no City write authority. Verify the real scoped action and its receipt. Live extension-to-farm capture, credential exchange and registry hosting still require their respective integration tests.

Return a chronicle with sources, scope, corrections, failures and a reviewable artefact. Agree before publishing private material, submitting benchmark work or spending resources. Never turn a proposed integration into a claim of deployment.

Funding semantics: Compute, Memory, Connection, Delegation and Protection provide the stable functional categories. Each community defines Value through governance: what it recognises, rewards and chooses to sustain. Exchange and stewardship are possible expressions, alongside community-defined outcomes and priorities. Record the community, its Value definition, agreed criteria, evidence and policy revision with each allocation. Communities can compare the five shared categories; their Value criteria need an explicit agreement before being treated as equivalent. Changes apply through a recorded governance decision, with existing commitments handled under their agreed terms.

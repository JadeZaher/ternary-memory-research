Hosting is the next step.</p><p>Support a dedicated Linux host, persistent storage and the work to bring the registry online. The intended home is <strong>kappa.mages.city</strong>; the service is not running yet.</p><figure class="project-bits"><div class="bit-sequence" role="img" aria-label="Protection: active; Delegation: inactive; Memory: active; Connection: active; Compute: inactive; Value: inactive"><span class="bit-cell on"><small>Prot</small><b>1</b></span><span class="bit-cell off"><small>Deleg</small><b>0</b></span><span class="bit-cell on"><small>Mem</small><b>1</b></span><span class="bit-cell on"><small>Conn</small><b>1</b></span><span class="bit-cell off"><small>Comp</small><b>0</b></span><span class="bit-cell off"><small>Value</small><b>0</b></span></div><figcaption><code>101100</code> · V44 · proposed need profile</figcaption></figure><section class="project-genesis-detail"><h3>Initial genesis allocation · 6%</h3><p>Registry hosting, storage, recovery and scoped integration are a shared-service dependency.</p><div class="project-need-highlights" aria-label="Relevant project dimensions"><span>Memory</span><span>Connection</span><span>Protection</span></div><p>Highlighted terms show the needs this project serves. Specific allocations follow the agreed scope and community governance.</p><div class="milestone-illustration"><h3>At the first $420k milestone · $25,200</h3><p>If the milestone were fully funded and the initial sketch adopted, this project’s 6% share would represent $25,200. Each Star pool receives this envelope multiplied by its reviewed weight.</p><p>The proposed 15% maintainer provision is $3,780, included across the relevant pools. It is not additional funding.</p><p class="dim">A planning illustration, not an available balance, separate project target or final foundation requirement.</p></div><h3>Maintainers &amp; First Persons</h3><p>The included 15% reserve supports continuity, scoped work and review across this project’s active dimensions. Roles, six-way attribution and compensation require agreement.</p><a href="/fund/#allocation-maths">See the six-coordinate allocation model →</a></section><div class="need-profile"><h3>What this work needs</h3><p>Memory · Connection · Protection</p><p class="dim">Proposed Star need profile, based on this brief’s scope. Workshops review the profile and offer capacity; these categories do not set a budget or grant authority.</p></div><details class="project-resource-details"><summary>How shared pools support this work</summary><ul><li><a href="/fund/#pool-memory">Memory</a> — Store selected readings and maintain persistence, backups and recovery.</li><li><a href="/fund/#pool-connection">Connection</a> — Connect the City registry to Star Key and participating services.</li><li><a href="/fund/#pool-protection">Protection</a> — Verify content read-back and test scoped access to stored material.</li></ul><p>The project proposes its needs and intended public benefit. Allocation is considered through the proposed <a href="/fund/#allocation">Mages City governance process</a>, alongside the needs of other projects. Shared costs are counted once; there is no separate project fundraising target.</p></details><h3>What support makes possible</h3><ol><li>Run the pinned UOR registry on persistent Linux infrastructure.</li><li>Register a test Star reading and verify its content address on retrieval.</li><li>Check access restrictions, backups and persistence across restarts.</li><li>Open the City endpoint, then develop VTA-signed bindings and scoped community access.</li></ol><details><summary>Built so far</summary><p>Star Key derives a κ address for a selected reading and exports a registry entry.

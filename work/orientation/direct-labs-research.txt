    <span class="section-kicker">Three thrusts</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">Separation, <em>identity</em>, value.</h2><details class="section-disclosure"><summary>Explore this work</summary>
    <div class="hair c3">
      <div class="node"><span class="node-label coral">T1 · separation and time</span><span class="node-name">What does separation buy, and for how long?</span><span class="node-text">The conditional reconstruction ceiling and its wiretap provenance; the moving ceiling <span class="mono" style="color:var(--cyan);font-size:13px">R(t)</span> and shelf life; the leakage gap between policy-separated and amnesia-separated agent chains. Methods: information-theoretic proof, benchmark measurement, longitudinal case analysis.</span></div>
      <div class="node cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">T2 · protocol and identity</span><span class="node-name">Can understanding be the root of identity?</span><span class="node-text">The relationship proverb protocol, verifiable relationship credentials, proof-of-understanding recovery, hash-locked selective disclosure, the dual-agent pattern as an implementation of published standards. Methods: protocol specification, adversarial evaluation against forgery, replay, Sybil and compression-gaming.</span></div>
      <div class="node indigo"><span class="node-label">T3 · value and governance</span><span class="node-name">What is behavioural sovereignty worth?</span><span class="node-text">The seventh-capital thesis, the data-value gap, reputation economics of trust tiers, invitation versus imposition as market structure, the regulatory substrate. Methods: valuation with stated assumptions, mechanism design, comparative policy, standards participation as fieldwork.</span></div>

    <span class="section-kicker coral">The agenda</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">Eleven extraction areas, <em>one register</em>.</h2>
    <p class="lede">Every artefact the programme produces traces to a line in one of these extractions, and every extraction line to a canon section or a verified record. Trace or delete.</p>
    <div class="hair c4">
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label coral">E1</span><span class="node-name">The amnesia gap</span><span class="node-text">Structural forgetting as a primitive: the witness is gone, not hidden. Policy-separated versus amnesia-separated chains.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">E2</span><span class="node-name">The moving ceiling</span><span class="node-text">Guarantees carry expiry dates. <span class="mono" style="color:var(--cyan);font-size:12.5px">R(t)</span>, shelf life <span class="mono" style="color:var(--cyan);font-size:12.5px">t*</span>, the behavioural form of the migration inequality.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">E3</span><span class="node-name">The relationship proverb protocol</span><span class="node-text">Understanding as key. Compression that preserves the operative constraints, exchanged bilaterally, inscribed on Zcash.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight amber"><span class="node-label amber">E4</span><span class="node-name">The seventh capital</span><span class="node-text">Behavioural data as capital. The value gap between sovereign and surveillance architectures, and the tier and mana mechanics that price it.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label coral">E5</span><span class="node-name">The algebraic home</span><span class="node-text">The sovereignty lattice as a ring, <span class="mono" style="color:var(--cyan);font-size:12.5px">neg ∘ bnot = succ</span>; the UOR convergence; the parity cube and the octahedral gap.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">E6</span><span class="node-name">Promise-theoretic agent governance</span><span class="node-text">The autonomy axiom as the reason for two agents rather than one; the integrity-gap convergence.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">E7</span><span class="node-name">Identity architecture and VRCs</span><span class="node-text">Three identity layers, content-addressed; verifiable relationship credentials; the trust-graph specifications at Trust over IP.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight amber"><span class="node-label amber">E8</span><span class="node-name">Governance by ceremony</span><span class="node-text">Positions filled by demonstrated understanding rather than election: bilateral trust ceremonies, the Game of 42, the heptad.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label coral">E9</span><span class="node-name">The proving substrate</span><span class="node-text">Keys as accumulators, folding schemes, binary-field provers, the post-quantum thread; zero-knowledge as operational experience, not only theory.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">E10</span><span class="node-name">The method record</span><span class="node-text">The gates, the reading ledger, the audits, the propagation checklist: how the programme holds itself to its own rules, as a subject in its own right.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">E11</span><span class="node-name">The implementation record</span><span class="node-text">The City Key reference implementation, the byte-exact seal, the site library: the code that stands behind the claims.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight amber"><span class="node-label amber">venues</span><span class="node-name">Where it is aimed</span><span class="node-text">PoPETs and SaTML for separation and time; CPP or ITP for the mechanisation; SOUPS or Financial Cryptography for the protocol's adversarial analysis; WEIS for the seventh capital; ePrint for priority. Papers in progress are not linked here.</span></div>
    </div>
    <p class="dim" style="margin-top:22px;font-size:14px;max-width:900px">Published expressions of the model live at <a href="https://agentprivacy.ai/model" rel="noopener">agentprivacy.ai/model</a>, the practitioner guide at <a href="https://guide.agentprivacy.ai" rel="noopener">guide.agentprivacy.ai</a>, and the essays at <a href="https://sync.soulbis.com" rel="noopener">sync.soulbis.com</a>. <section class="section">
  <div class="wrap" style="padding:0">
    <span class="section-kicker coral">Autoresearch</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">The harness <em>across the corpus</em>.</h2><details class="section-disclosure"><summary>Explore this work</summary>
    <p class="lede">The same separation that protects a person runs as a research method. One agent proposes; a second refuses to promote anything it cannot independently reproduce against witnesses drawn from the proposal itself, hashed with a secret the proposer never sees. A round ends in a <em>fold</em>: the frontier of claims shrinks to what survived, and what was ruled out is filed with its grounds. Value is what survives disclosure to a validator you cannot tune. These are folds the harness has made, with their numbers as recorded.</p>
    <div class="hair c3">
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label coral">the programme itself</span><span class="node-name">The rehydration fleet</span><span class="node-text">Fourteen roles, one per session, five gates, four deterministic checks, an append-only critique ledger. Three fleet cycles in a single day. Literature sweeps as proposer, refuter and judge: run three closed with one claim covered, four narrowed, none surviving and seventy-four bibliography additions; run four narrowed both of its legs.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">the canon</span><span class="node-name">Three census folds</span><span class="node-text">The trust-graph credential specification 980 → 863, the delegation agent's canon 1248 → 1072, the boundary agent's 1397 → 1154: twelve claims validated, no mirage, across the three.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">standards · BGIN</span><span class="node-name">The community security agent</span><span class="node-text">Twenty findings against the public agent, folded 808 → 701 → 665 at fifty-one of fifty-one checks, one round per fold; the winning moves filed as pull requests upstream.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight amber"><span class="node-label amber">standards · ToIP</span><span class="node-name">The gate, run by other hands</span><span class="node-text">Two hundred and fourteen assertions green; a collaborator ran the admission gate unmodified end to end, thirty-three of thirty-three, and his tests folded in, sixty-four to seventy-two.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label coral">the grammar</span><span class="node-name">Lexon, the controlled-English workshop</span><span class="node-text">The privacy model in a legal-English subset: coverage debt 211 → 22 over five rounds, nine candidate grammar levers, one performative false friend caught.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">a lending protocol</span><span class="node-name">The Logos research instance</span><span class="node-text">Seventy-five seats over five rounds: five validated, fifteen mirage, none blocked; the proposal's frontier compressed 737 → 595 words and its privacy rules mapped one to one onto the model's collapses.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">a convergence</span><span class="node-name">The UOR kappa lane</span><span class="node-text">Twelve seats, one per connection, twelve holds-with-corrections; six thesis notes refuter-tested: none survived intact, six narrowed, four covered, and the mint thesis defeated and folded.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight amber"><span class="node-label amber">an inference seam</span><span class="node-name">Kwaai's KwaaiNet and VPK</span><span class="node-text">Inference-side disclosure findings verified against source and binaries; a second run showed that scale does not fix the inversion, and one finding was observed on the wire.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label coral">a field guide</span><span class="node-name">The convergence merge</span><span class="node-text">A collaborator's field machine merged into the lab's suite: one hundred and twenty-four of one hundred and twenty-four suites green on Windows, byte-identical vectors.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">a cohort</span><span class="node-name">The workshop harness</span><span class="node-text">A standalone harness for a masters cohort: twenty-six of twenty-six grammar checks, twenty of twenty declared claims, and the critic holding the fold when the frontier had nothing left to give.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">public benchmarks</span><span class="node-name">Where the referee cannot be tuned</span><span class="node-text">The ecdsa.fail board: the frontier reproduced, eight candidate optimisations refused in ordinary Python before any accelerated compute (public as <a href="https://github.com/mitchuski/shor_mage" rel="noopener">shor_mage</a>). Yukon research lanes include sparse-matrix ordering, a modular-exponentiation precompile with kernel-checked Lean proofs, and proximity-testing bounds. The matrices_mage instance has produced promoted frontier advances on matrices.fast. <a href="/fund/#matrices">Inspect the public results and harness attribution.</a></span></div>
      <div class="node tight amber"><span class="node-label amber">the knowledge base</span><span class="node-name">A dream cycle</span><span class="node-text">A machine-wide inventory folded into a working knowledge base for the identity and key-management group: canonical copies, dedupe rulings, the first local archive. The harness as librarian.</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="aside"><b>The envelope.</b> Benchmark toolchains are other participants' work; the harness reads each arena's score rather than computing it; no headline is claimed on any board; and results delivered privately to a partner are not counted here. What is demonstrated is a method and a set of negative results, each filed in a kill ledger that says what was ruled out, on what grounds, and whether the ruling was new.</div>
  </div>
</details></section>

    <span class="section-kicker">How we work</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">Evidence <em>discipline</em>.</h2>
    <div class="hair c3">
      <div class="node tight"><span class="node-label">unreproduced results stay in</span><span class="node-text">Findings are held out of all outgoing communication until independently reproduced, including findings that would be commercially useful to announce. This costs marketing tempo and buys the ability to be believed.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight cyan"><span class="node-label cyan">the register wins</span><span class="node-text">A single conjecture register numbers the whole body of work. Collaborator and machine proposals enter under a candidate series until assigned. Corrections are made in the specification, not in the prose describing it.</span></div>
      <div class="node tight indigo"><span class="node-label">falsification, pre-registered</span><span class="node-text">What would prove the thesis wrong is written down before the work, and results are filed to the public register whichever way they fall. Verdict first, one named result per meeting.</span></div>
